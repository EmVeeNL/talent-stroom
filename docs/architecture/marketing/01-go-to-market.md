# Marketing 01 — Dutch-first go-to-market strategy

## Brand and message
Product name **TalentStroom** (Wervlo was the earlier working name); verify domain, Benelux/EU trademark and possible confusion before adoption. Tagline: **Werven zonder gedoe.** Alternative directional names require clearance, not assumed availability.

## Positioning
For recruitmentbureaus with 2–10 recruiters, TalentStroom is the Dutch-first recruitment CRM that replaces fragmented spreadsheets and overbuilt ATS tools: candidate management, vacancy pipelines, CV assistance, and polished client shortlists in one fast interface.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Minder administratie:** CV → structured profile with human control.
2. **Alles in één overzicht:** Candidates, vacancies, clients and tasks.
3. **Professionele voordrachten:** Invite clients to view secure, expiring shortlists without new accounts.
4. **Nederlandse werkwijze:** Dutch terms, local formatting, clear privacy controls.
5. **Beveiliging serieus genomen:** Sensitive records encrypted at app layer; precise claims only after review/testing.

## ICP and acquisition
Primary: specialized IT/engineering/finance recruitment boutiques in NL, 2–10 seats. Channels: founder-led outreach, niche LinkedIn content, recruiter communities/events, SEO for Dutch intent (`recruitment CRM`, `ATS voor recruitmentbureau`, `kandidaten beheren`, `cv parsing`, `vacature pipeline`), referrals, and partners providing workflow consulting.

## Funnel
Landing → demo video/product screenshots → beta application → qualification → guided onboarding → first vacancy/candidate/client link → feedback → activation → subscription. Instrument conversion without exposing applicant PII.

## Launch offers
Private beta invitation, assisted migration, usage-based trial constraints, straightforward per-seat or team pricing validated with customer interviews. Avoid promising unlimited AI parsing until unit economics are understood.

## Content plan (8 themes)
- Spreadsheet chaos vs an ATS, explained plainly.
- How to structure a recruitment pipeline for a 5-person bureau.
- Securely sharing CVs with opdrachtgevers.
- Dutch AVG candidate retention: guidance, not a legal shortcut.
- What AI can and can't safely do in recruitment.
- CV parsing with human review.
- How niche recruiting teams customize module layouts.
- What an agency should measure every week.

## KPIs
Qualified beta applications, demo-to-beta conversion, workspace activation, retained weekly active orgs, shortlist feedback rate, trial-to-paid, paid churn, customer acquisition cost and cost per parsed CV. Report cohort trends, not vanity views.

## Example Dutch hero
**Werven zonder gedoe.**
Beheer kandidaten, vacatures en opdrachtgevers op één plek. Maak in minuten een shortlist en ontvang feedback zonder eindeloze mailtjes.
CTA: **Vraag beta-toegang aan** · secondary: **Bekijk hoe het werkt**.
