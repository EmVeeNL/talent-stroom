# TalentStroom — design asset bundle

**Brand name:** TalentStroom. **Primary audience:** Dutch boutique recruitment agencies. **Primary font:** Asap Sharp. **UI icon set:** Google Material Symbols Outlined.

## What is included

- `screens/`: eight individual PNG screen crops from earlier AI-generated concept boards (login, pipeline, candidates, candidate profile, job details, clients, tasks, client portal).
- `overview/`: three original full overview screenshots/collages.
- `brand/`: **new TalentStroom** SVG wordmarks (light/dark) and a vector brand mark, plus a PNG mark if available. These are provisional original concepts, not trademark-vetted.
- `tokens/`: brand color tokens in JSON and CSS.
- `implementation/`: typography, Google Material Symbols, screen inventory.

## Honest visual status

Earlier screen renders include placeholder names including **TalentMate**, **TalentStroom**, or **RecruitFlow** and may include text/image-generation artifacts. They are **visual references, not rebranded production-ready designs**, and their typography/iconography do not consistently follow the final specification. The new vector logo assets use TalentStroom consistently. A clean UI rebuild in React/Vite should follow the tokens, Asap Sharp and Material Symbols instead of copying screenshot details literally.

## Licensing & restrictions

Asap Sharp (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and Material Symbols Outlined (Apache-2.0) are bundled under `packages/ui/fonts/` with their license files and source revisions. Do not treat example companies/candidates in mockups as real endorsements or usable stock imagery.
